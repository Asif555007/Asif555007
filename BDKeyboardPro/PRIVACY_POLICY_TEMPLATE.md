# BD Keyboard Pro — Privacy Policy (template; edit before publishing)

**What stays on your device:** typing, learned words, snippets and clipboard history (if you turn it on). Nothing you type is logged or sent for analytics.

**What is sent, and only when you ask:**
- *AI writing tools / translation:* only the text you select or paste, after you tap an action, is sent over HTTPS to our server, which forwards it to an AI provider to produce the result. [State retention: e.g. "We do not store the text."]
- *Voice typing:* uses your device's speech recognition service. Audio may be processed online by that service (e.g. Google). The microphone is used only after you tap the mic.

**Password fields:** never learned, suggested, captured or sent.

**Private mode:** stops learning and clipboard capture inside this keyboard. It cannot hide text from the app you type into or the operating system.

**Your controls:** clear learned words and clipboard history, disable AI, uninstall to remove all local data.
